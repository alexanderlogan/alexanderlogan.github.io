# Interview 04 (fictional)
Interviewee: Lukas W., CIO, industrial manufacturer headquartered in Germany, ~5,000 office staff across EU and US
Interviewer: PM, Uplift
Date: (mock)

PM: What's your situation with AI tools?
Lukas: We run three: one vendor's assistant in the office suite, a coding agent for engineering, and a chat tool for everyone else. The board wants one number: is it paying off? I can't give it, because the usage data sits in three admin consoles with different definitions.

PM: What would a useful view look like?
Lukas: Adoption and value by function and by workflow, across all tools, normalized. Plus license utilization, so I stop paying for seats nobody uses.

PM: What constraints matter most?
Lukas: The works council must approve any system that could evaluate employee performance. Anything individual-level is a non-starter in Germany without their agreement. Data stays in the EU. Aggregation thresholds so you can't identify a person in a five-person team.

PM: How long would approval take?
Lukas: If it's designed for privacy from day one, a few months. If not, never.

PM: What would make you buy?
Lukas: Cross-tool visibility and a privacy design my works council signs off on quickly. Whoever solves that wins Europe.
