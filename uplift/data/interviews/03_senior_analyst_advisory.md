# Interview 03 (fictional)
Interviewee: Aisha B., Senior Analyst, mid-size accounting and advisory firm, ~2,000 employees
Interviewer: PM, Uplift
Date: (mock)

PM: How do you use AI in your work?
Aisha: Quietly. I use it for first drafts of client memos and to check spreadsheets. Honestly, I don't advertise it.

PM: Why not?
Aisha: Two reasons. If my manager sees I finish in two hours what used to take eight, I get six more hours of work, not credit. And there's a feeling that we're training the thing that replaces us. Half my team thinks that.

PM: How would you feel about a tool that measures AI adoption?
Aisha: Nervous, if it's about me individually. If it's at team level, anonymized, fine. And if it tells me something useful back, like "people in your role save time with this workflow, here's how," I'd actually use that.

PM: What would make you share what you've figured out?
Aisha: Recognition that counts. If sharing a good workflow showed up in my review as a contribution, I'd share everything. Right now there's no upside.

PM: Anything that would be a dealbreaker?
Aisha: Reading my prompts or documents. That's client confidential. Absolutely not.
