# Interview 01 (fictional)
Interviewee: Rachel M., VP Learning & Development, global insurance carrier, ~9,000 employees
Interviewer: PM, Uplift
Date: (mock)

PM: Where are you with AI tools today?
Rachel: We bought four thousand enterprise AI licenses eight months ago. Renewal is in March and finance is asking me what we got for the money. I can tell them how many people logged in. That's it.

PM: What would you want to know instead?
Rachel: Which workflows people actually use it for. Underwriting summaries? Claims letters? Email? If I knew where it sticks, I'd know where to put training. Right now I run the same generic prompt workshop for everyone and attendance is falling.

PM: How do you measure time saved today?
Rachel: A self-reported survey. People either ignore it or say "it saves me five hours a week," which nobody believes. I need something credible enough to put in front of the CFO.

PM: Who are your best users?
Rachel: I hear names anecdotally. There's a claims team in Ohio that apparently built their own prompt library. I found out by accident. I'd love to find those people systematically and turn them into champions.

PM: Any concerns?
Rachel: Our employee council will push back hard on anything that looks like monitoring individuals. If this turns into "the AI is watching you," adoption goes down, not up.
