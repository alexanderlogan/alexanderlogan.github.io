# Interview 02 (fictional)
Interviewee: Tom K., VP Product, B2B software company, ~120 product managers across 6 business units
Interviewer: PM, Uplift
Date: (mock)

PM: How are your product managers using AI?
Tom: Wildly unevenly. Maybe fifteen of them have built real workflows: research synthesis, PRD drafts, ticket breakdowns. They're shipping specs in a day that used to take a week. The other hundred mostly use it to rewrite emails.

PM: Why the gap?
Tom: The good ones figured it out alone and nobody else knows how. When I ask them to share, they send a forty-page prompt doc nobody reads. Knowledge doesn't spread.

PM: What would help?
Tom: Show me which workflows produce real results, then package them so other teams can pick them up. A library of proven workflows, with the person who built it getting credit. Like an internal app store for how we work.

PM: How would you know it's working?
Tom: Cycle time from discovery to approved spec, and how often engineering bounces a spec back. If those move, I don't care about logins.

PM: Anything you'd avoid?
Tom: Leaderboards. The moment I rank PMs by AI usage, people will game it with junk prompts.
